Dots and Lines

A pencil & paper classic

Dots and Lines is a mobile game of the timeless dots-and-boxes strategy duel. Draw a line between two dots on your turn; close the fourth side of a box to claim it and go again. Whoever claims the most boxes wins.

  • Play against a smart AI across four difficulty levels
  • Pass-and-play locally with a friend on one device
  • Challenge friends online with a shareable room code
  • Tournament series, undo, themes, and more
