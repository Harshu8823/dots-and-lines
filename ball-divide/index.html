Ball Divide

Keep the balls apart by building walls between them.

  • Tap or click to fire two dots out from that point. They draw a wall as they travel to the edges.
  • If a ball touches the wall while it's still being drawn, the game is over.
  • Once every ball sits in its own section, you clear the level. Each level adds one more ball.
  • Switch between vertical and horizontal walls with the button, Space, or right-click.
