Ball Divide

Tap between the balls

  • Tap to fire two dots out from that point. They draw a wall until they reach the edge.
  • Swipe up/down or left/right to pick the wall's direction, or use the button, Space, or right-click.
  • A ball touching an unfinished wall costs a life. You have 3.
  • Give every ball its own space to clear the level. Each level adds a ball, and later levels bring new balls and goals.
