Containment protocol

Ball Confine

Tap beside the orbs

  • Tap to charge a force field. Two emitters race out from that point until they reach the walls.
  • Swipe up/down or left/right to pick the field's direction, or use the button, Space, or right-click.
  • Chambers with no orbs get sealed. Shrink the orbs' chamber below the containment threshold to clear the level.
  • Keep the orbs together. A field that splits them is a containment breach, and an orb hitting a field that is still charging overloads it. Either costs a life. You have 3.
  • From level 4, antimatter shows up. Wall it off on its own; it can't be in the orbs' chamber when you finish.
