Ball Confine

Tap beside the balls

  • Tap to fire two dots out from that point. They draw a wall until they reach the edge.
  • Swipe up/down or left/right to pick the wall's direction, or use the button, Space, or right-click.
  • Any section with no balls in it is filled in. Shrink the balls' space down to the target to clear the level.
  • Keep the balls together. A wall that splits them apart breaks, and a ball touching an unfinished wall also costs a life. You have 3.
