Privacy Policy

For the “Dots and Lines” mobile game · Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the “Dots and Lines” mobile app (“the App”, “we”) handles information. The App is created by an independent developer. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we handle

The App does not require an account and never asks for your real name, email, phone number, or precise location. The limited data involved is:

  • On-device settings. The player names, theme, and sound/animation preferences you choose are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Online multiplayer. If you play an online match, the App uses Google Firebase to create a temporary anonymous ID and to sync game data (the names you type, moves, and room codes) between you and your opponent. This is tied to a random room code, not to your identity, and the names you enter are visible to your opponent in that room.
  • Purchases. If you buy “Remove Ads”, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing and validated through RevenueCat. We receive only a confirmation and an app-generated anonymous identifier — never your card or payment details.
  • Advertising. Unless you have removed ads, the App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads.

2. Third-party services

The App relies on these services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play services — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat — revenuecat.com/privacy

3. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

4. Data retention

On-device data stays until you clear it or uninstall the App. Online-match data stored via Firebase is temporary and expires once the match or room ends.

5. Your choices

  • You can limit personalized ads through your device’s ad settings.
  • You can purchase “Remove Ads” to stop seeing ads entirely.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all on-device data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
